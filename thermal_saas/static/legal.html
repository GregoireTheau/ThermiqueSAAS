1. What ThermalTwin is — and is not

ThermalTwin is a sales-support tool for roof-insulation businesses. It provides thermal estimates and simulations comparing simplified before-and-after cases using user-provided property information and selected historical or typical weather data.

Outputs are indicative estimates only. They are not a home energy rating, HERS rating, energy audit, engineering calculation, load calculation, inspection, appraisal, code or permit determination, tax-credit certification, DPE, or other regulatory or certified assessment. ThermalTwin does not inspect the property and does not verify user inputs.

Actual energy use, savings, costs, temperatures, and comfort can differ materially because of weather, occupant behavior, utility tariffs, workmanship, air leakage, moisture, equipment operation, ducts, building geometry, and conditions that the model does not represent. No result is a guarantee, warranty, quotation, or promise of performance.
